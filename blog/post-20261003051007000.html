Foundry Spotlight: Lundeen Sculpture Studios

Lundeen Sculpture Studios, at 338 E. 4th St. in downtown Loveland, is a good first foundry profile for two reasons: it's a genuine multi-generation family

Lundeen Sculpture Studios, at 338 E. 4th St. in downtown Loveland, is a good first foundry profile for two reasons: it's a genuine multi-generation family operation, and its founder is directly tied to the story of Sculpture in the Park itself.

George Lundeen founded the studio in 1976; his brother Mark joined in 1981, and it's grown into a family-operated business employing roughly 30 people, including multiple family members — George, Mark, Cammie Lundeen, and Bets Holland Lundeen among them. Their work spans collector pieces and large-scale public art installations.

The studio is open to visitors Monday–Friday, 9:30 AM–3:00 PM; they encourage calling ahead at (970) 669-7176 to check availability or arrange a visit, since the studio doesn't run a formal guided-tour program — think "visit the studio and gallery" rather than a scheduled foundry tour.

See the George Lundeen artist spotlight for more on the sculptor behind the studio and Sculpture in the Park's founding story.
