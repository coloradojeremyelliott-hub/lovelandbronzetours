Loveland Sculpture Garden Walking Tour: A Quiet Fall Visit

Why fall is the best time to walk Benson Sculpture Garden, plus the real history behind how it started.

Loveland Sculpture Garden Walking Tour: A Quiet Fall Visit

Benson Sculpture Garden gets its heaviest foot traffic in early August during Sculpture in the Park, but the garden itself is there year-round — and fall is arguably the better time to actually walk it.

The garden dates back to 1984, when five local sculptors — George Lundeen, Dan Ostermiller, George Walbye, Fritz White, and Hollis Williford — partnered with the City of Loveland to launch what became Sculpture in the Park. The city designated Benson Park as the permanent site the following year. What started as a single show has grown into a standing collection: the garden now holds 189 sculptures valued at over $5 million.

Fewer crowds, cooler weather, and the bronze work reads differently against turning leaves than it does in peak summer light — bring a camera.
