Artist Spotlight: George Lundeen

If Loveland Bronze Tours profiles one artist first, George Lundeen is the strongest choice — he's one of the five sculptors who founded Sculpture in the Park in 1984, and he's still working out of his family's Loveland studio today.

In 1984, Lundeen and four other local sculptors — Dan Ostermiller, George Walbye, Fritz White, and Hollis Williford — partnered with city officials to put on an outdoor sculpture show. That first year drew 50 artists and 2,000 visitors and generated $50,000 in sales, enough to convince the city to permanently designate Benson Park as a sculpture garden the following year. More than 1,000 different artists have shown work there since, and the show now generates over $1 million in annual sales, making it the largest outdoor juried sculpture show in the country.

Born in 1948 in Holdrege, Nebraska, Lundeen founded his Loveland studio in 1976 after working as a teacher and at foundries both domestically and abroad. Southwest Art magazine has credited him with helping make "Loveland into the sculpture capital of the West." His public commissions, worth naming directly:

  • Benjamin Franklin — a figure of Franklin installed at the University of Pennsylvania, with additional castings placed across the US, Mexico, France, and England.
  • Departure — a sculpture in Greenwood Village, Colorado, inspired by a sketch of travelers in an Italian train station.
  • The Joy of Music — a five-figure bronze ensemble (Lundeen included himself in it, playing bass), with installations in Loveland and elsewhere.
  • Promise of the Prairie — a family-group sculpture in his hometown of Holdrege, Nebraska, and one of his earliest commercial successes.
  • Stations of the Cross — a multi-sculpture installation along a 2,500-foot walkway at the Cloisters on the Platte near Omaha, Nebraska.
  • Two statues Lundeen sculpted represent Colorado and Kansas in the National Statuary Hall Collection in the U.S. Capitol.

Visit the studio where he still works, or see the garden he helped start.
