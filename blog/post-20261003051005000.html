Loveland's Downtown Art Walk: A Self-Guided Guide

Loveland's downtown is small enough to cover on foot in an afternoon, and the Downtown Art Walk is the easiest way to see why the town calls itself an art town and means it. It's free, self-guided, and covers five stops within a few blocks of each other:

  • Art Space Loveland / Sidetrack Gallery (140 W 3rd St)
  • Artworks Center for Contemporary Art (310 N Railroad Ave)
  • Independence Gallery (233 E 4th St)
  • Lincoln Gallery (429 N Lincoln Ave)
  • Loveland Museum (503 N Lincoln Ave)

There's no set schedule or event date attached to it — this isn't a once-a-year festival, it's a year-round route you can walk any weekend. That makes it a good fit for shoulder-season visitors specifically: quieter downtown, no festival crowds, and the galleries themselves are the draw rather than an outdoor event competing for attention.

A practical way to plan the walk: start near parking, work your way through the five stops in roughly the order above, and budget more time at whichever gallery's current show interests you most — shows rotate, so call ahead if a specific exhibit matters to you. Pair it with a visit to Lundeen Sculpture Studios and Benson Sculpture Garden if you want more after the walk.
