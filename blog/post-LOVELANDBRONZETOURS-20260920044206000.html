Loveland's Downtown Art Walk: A Self-Guided Guide

Loveland's downtown is small enough to cover on foot in an afternoon, and the Downtown Art Walk is the easiest way to see why the town calls itself an art town and means it.

It's free, self-guided, and covers five stops within a few blocks of each other:

  • Art Space Loveland / Sidetrack Gallery — 140 W 3rd St
  • Artworks Center for Contemporary Art — 310 N Railroad Ave
  • Independence Gallery — 233 E 4th St
  • Lincoln Gallery — 429 N Lincoln Ave
  • Loveland Museum — 503 N Lincoln Ave

There's no set schedule or event date attached to it — it's a year-round route you can walk any weekend, which makes it a good fit for a quieter, off-peak visit. Pair it with a foundry tour for a fuller day of Loveland's art scene.
