Sculpture in the Park 2026: A Recap

Sculpture in the Park 2026 ran August 7–9 in Benson Sculpture Garden — billed as the largest outdoor juried sculpture show in the country, with annual sales that have topped $1 million in recent years.

Benson Sculpture Garden itself now holds 189 sculptures valued at over $5 million, the standing collection built up since the show's first year in 1984.
