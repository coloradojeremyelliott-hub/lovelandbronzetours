Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Last updated: September 27, 2026

This policy explains what information Loveland Bronze Tours (lovelandbronzetours.com) collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. We keep it short because we collect very little.

Information we collect

  • Basic technical data. Like almost every website, our hosting provider (Netlify) automatically records standard server logs when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested, and the time of the request. These are used for security and to keep the site running.
  • Emails you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include, and use it only to reply.

We don't require accounts, and we don't ask for sensitive information.

How we use it

  • To run, secure, and improve the site.
  • To reply to messages and requests you send us.

We do not sell your personal information, and we don't add you to a mailing list unless you ask to join one.

Cookies and advertising

This site shows ads served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info. To learn more, see how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

If you visit from a region that requires consent for these cookies (such as the EU or UK), you'll be asked before personalized ads are shown.

Some pages contain affiliate links. If you click one, the retailer may use cookies to credit us with a small commission if you buy something, at no extra cost to you.

Services we use

We share information only with the services that help us run the site, and only what they need:

  • Netlify hosts the site.
  • Google Fonts delivers the site's typefaces, which means your browser connects to Google's servers.
  • Google AdSense shows ads, as described above.

We may also disclose information if required by law.

How long we keep it

Server logs are kept by our host for a limited time. Messages are kept only as long as needed to respond and follow up.

Your choices

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it (except records we must keep by law). To make a request, email us at hello@lovelandbronzetours.com. Depending on where you live (for example, California, Colorado, or the EU), you may have additional rights under local law, and we'll honor them.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Questions? Email us at hello@lovelandbronzetours.com.
