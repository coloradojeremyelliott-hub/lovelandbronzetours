Self-Guided

Walk Loveland's public art, free and on your own schedule

You don't need a tour reservation or an August trip to see Loveland's sculpture scene. This is a loose, three-stop route you can do in an afternoon — a permanent sculpture garden, a downtown gallery walk, and a sculpture park at a shopping center most visitors drive right past.

Benson Sculpture Garden — photo by Ijon, CC BY-SA 4.0

1
Benson Sculpture Garden — 2908 Aspen Drive

Start here. Ten acres, 188 permanent bronze sculptures worth an estimated $5 million, free and open essentially year-round. Paved, handicap-accessible paths loop the whole garden, with a public restroom and several picnic areas along the way — easy to spend an hour just wandering. An expanded section continues across 29th Street.

2
Downtown Art Walk — centered around 3rd & 4th Streets

A free, self-guided walk connecting five galleries within easy walking distance of each other: Art Space Loveland / Sidetrack Gallery (140 W 3rd St), Artworks Center for Contemporary Art (310 N Railroad Ave), Independence Gallery (233 E 4th St), Lincoln Gallery (429 N Lincoln Ave), and the Loveland Museum (503 N Lincoln Ave). Expect everything from contemporary exhibitions to historic collections and emerging local artists — plenty of it bronze, some not.

3
Chapungu Sculpture Park — Promenade Shops at Centerra

A less-expected stop: a sculpture park set inside an outdoor shopping center on Loveland's east side, worth a short detour if you're already near Centerra for food or shopping.

Go Deeper

The city has an app for this

Loveland's Art in Public Places program covers 581 works citywide, valued at roughly $12.9 million — far more than any one walk can cover. Two free resources help you explore beyond this route:

Otocast app

A free app (iOS and Android) with audio interviews with the artists, photos, and turn-by-turn directions to public art pieces across town. Search "Loveland, CO" inside the app to load the local tour.

Citywide public art map

A printable PDF map of public art locations across Loveland's Creative District, published by the city's public art program — handy if you'd rather plan your own route than follow ours.

Good to know: outside of Sculpture in the Park's show weekend in August, Benson Garden and the downtown galleries are free and open on your own schedule — no tickets, no tour reservations, no crowds to plan around.
