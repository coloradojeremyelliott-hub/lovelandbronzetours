About

An independent guide, built by one person

Loveland Bronze Tours isn't run by the city, the visitors bureau, or either foundry — it's an independently produced visitor's guide focused specifically on Loveland's bronze foundries and public sculpture scene.

Why this site exists

Loveland's foundry tours and Sculpture in the Park are well-documented individually, but scattered across the foundries' own sites, the official tourism board, and event listings. This site pulls the logistics — tour days, ticket prices, parking, timing — into one place, plus the kind of practical, opinionated tips a tourism board doesn't usually publish (which foundry to pick, when to actually show up).

How we keep it accurate

Tour schedules, prices, and event dates are drawn from the foundries' and event organizers' own published information and checked periodically. These things change — always confirm directly with Art Castings of Colorado, Bronze Services of Loveland, or the Loveland High Plains Arts Council before finalizing your plans.

How This Site Is Supported

Advertising & affiliate disclosure

Loveland Bronze Tours is supported by display advertising (Google AdSense) and may include travel affiliate links (for example, hotel booking links) that earn a small commission if you book through them, at no extra cost to you. This never affects which foundries, events, or places we cover or how we describe them.

Get in Touch

Spot an error, or run one of these foundries?

Tour times and prices change without much notice. If something on this site is out of date, or you're affiliated with a foundry, gallery, or event listed here and want to suggest an update, reach out at hello@lovelandbronzetours.com.
