Loveland, Colorado

Your independent guide to Sculpture City, USA

Loveland is home to two working bronze foundries that open their doors to the public, 188 permanent sculptures in one ten-acre garden, and the largest outdoor juried sculpture show in the country. This is the guide the tourism board doesn't have time to write — foundry tour logistics, insider timing for Sculpture in the Park, and a self-guided walk through the art itself.

Why Loveland

A working art town, not just a museum piece

Most sculpture towns show you finished bronze in a park. Loveland lets you watch it get made.

🔥

Real, working foundries

Art Castings of Colorado and Bronze Services of Loveland are active production foundries — not recreations — and both run small, hands-on public tours through the lost-wax casting process.

🌳

188 sculptures, one garden

Benson Sculpture Garden holds 188 pieces of permanent, publicly owned bronze across ten free-to-visit acres — open year-round, no ticket required most of the year.

🎟️

The nation's largest juried show

Sculpture in the Park, held every August in Benson Garden, is the largest outdoor juried sculpture show and sale in the United States — with a Friday patron preview most visitors don't know about.

Good to Know

Loveland by the numbers

581+

Publicly owned artworks citywide through Loveland's Art in Public Places program, valued at roughly $12.9 million.

188

Permanent sculptures on display in Benson Sculpture Garden alone, worth an estimated $5 million.

42nd

Sculpture in the Park's 2026 show is its 42nd annual year running in Benson Garden every August.
